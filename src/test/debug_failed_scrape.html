About the job

Job Description

  • Proof-of-Concept development: Design and implement workplace-efficiency PoCs on our AI-agent platform.
  • Prompt engineering: Develop, test, and refine prompts to improve model accuracy and usability.
  • Python development: Write clean, maintainable Python code for PoC integrations, tooling, and dashboards.
  • Data structures & algorithms: Apply DSA principles to optimise code performance and model interactions.
  • Reg LLM report automation & generation: Build and maintain automated weekly/monthly performance reports and dashboards for leadership visibility.
  • Data accuracy & insights: validate key metrics, surface insights, and streamline reporting workflows.
  • Collaborate with product owners and end-users for rapid iteration and feedback loops.
  • Provide support on any other tasks assigned by the reporting manager.
Requirements
  • Currently pursuing a bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or related disciplines.
  • Strong proficiency in Python; familiarity with LLMs, NLP libraries, and prompt-engineering techniques is a plus.
  • Demonstrated understanding of data structures and algorithms (coursework, projects, or competitions).
  • Experience with version control, REST APIs, or basic cloud services is advantageous.
  • Prefer full-time interns; outstanding part-time candidates (≥3 days/week) will be considered

Shopee is a leading e-commerce platform in Southeast Asia, Taiwan and Brazil. It is a platform tailored for the region, providing customers with an easy, secure and fast online shopping experience through strong payment and logistical support. Shopee aims to continually enhance its platform and become the region’s e-commerce destination of choice via ongoing product optimisation and localised user-centred strategies. Shopee, a Sea company, was first launched in Singapore in 2015, and has since expanded its reach to Malaysia, Thailand, Taiwan, Indonesia, Vietnam and the Philippines. Sea is a leader in digital entertainment, e-commerce and digital financial services across Greater Southeast Asia. Sea's mission is to better the lives of consumers and small businesses with technology, and is listed on the NYSE under the symbol SE. The Shopee team is rapidly expanding across the region and we are constantly on the lookout for talents who have the passion and drive to become part of our dynamic team.
